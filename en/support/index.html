FAQ

Where is my data stored?

Only on your device. There is no account, and nothing is sent to a server.

Can I move my data to a new iPhone?

The App has no export feature. Your data is included in your iPhone backup (iCloud or computer), so it carries over if you restore your new device from a backup or transfer with Quick Start. Anything you recorded after your last backup will not carry over, so please back up your iPhone before switching devices.

What happens if I delete the App?

All App data stored on your device is deleted and cannot be recovered.

I bought Pro but it isn't active / I switched devices

Go to Settings → Pro and tap "Restore purchase." You need to be signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy it.

I'd like a refund

Purchases are made through the App Store, so please request a refund from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

I want my month to start on payday

Go to Settings → Month start day and choose any day from the 1st to the 28th.

How do I switch between dollars and yen?

Go to Settings → Currency. Only the symbol changes; amounts are not converted.

If I hide a category, are its past records deleted?

No. It just stops appearing on Home and the entry screen. Its records stay in History and Charts.
