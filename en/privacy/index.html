Privacy Policy

How Sakureco handles your information

This Privacy Policy explains how the developer of Sakureco ("we") handles information in the iOS app "Sakureco" (the "App").

1. Our approach

The App does not collect personal information. There is no account system, and we never receive information that identifies you.

2. Information in the App

The spending records you enter (amount, date, category, and memo), your category settings, and app settings such as your budget are stored only on your device. This information is never sent to our servers or to any third party.

3. Analytics, ads, and tracking

The App does not use analytics tools, advertising, crash report uploads, or any other tracking.

4. In-app purchases

In-app purchases such as Pro and tips are processed through Apple's App Store. Payment information is handled by Apple, and we never receive your credit card or other payment details. Please refer to Apple's Privacy Policy for how Apple handles your information.

5. When you contact us

If you contact us by email, we use your email address and message only to reply and respond to your inquiry. We do not share them with third parties except as required by law.

6. Deleting your data

Deleting the App from your device removes all data the App stored on it. Note that your device backups to iCloud or a computer may include the App's data.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as needed. The updated policy takes effect when it is posted on this page.
