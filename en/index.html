Just what you spent, roughly.

Sakureco is not a ledger you keep to the last cent. It is a simple spending log for people who only want to know what they spent on this month, how much, and how often.

  • Log in two taps: tap a category, type the amount
  • Monthly trends and a breakdown by category
  • No account needed. Your data stays on your device
